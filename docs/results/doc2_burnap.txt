MODERN TRANSLATION:
---
I, Thomas Burnap of (estate/place illegible in scan), yeoman, in the county of
Hertford, being of sound mind and memory, considering the uncertainty of this
life and the certainty of death, and in order to avoid disputes after my
death, make this my last will and testament as follows: I commit my soul to
Almighty God through the merits and mediation of his ever-blessed son Jesus
Christ, and my body to the earth to be decently buried at the discretion of
my executrix named below. As to my worldly estate, I give and bequeath all my
goods, chattels and personal estate, of whatever kind and wherever found, to
my loving wife Elizabeth Burnap, and I constitute and appoint my said loving
wife [my whole and sole executrix].
